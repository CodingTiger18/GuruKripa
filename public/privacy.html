Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Welcome to GuruKripa. We are committed to uncompromised digital privacy and adherence to global data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA, and Google AdSense policies).

1. Zero Server-Side PII Storage

GuruKripa operates with zero server-side databases. Your physical location coordinates, selected city, Japa Mala bead counters, favorite stotras, and theme preferences are stored strictly inside your local browser memory (via localStorage and IndexedDB). We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

2. Geolocation Processing

When you choose "Detect My Location" on the Panchang screen, coordinates are processed 100% locally on your device by our client-side astronomical algorithms. No geographic logs are sent to or stored on our servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

GuruKripa uses Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive contextual advertisements that support our zero-cost, free-access mission:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to GuruKripa and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out Page.

4. Affiliate Disclosures (Amazon Associates & Dharmic Publishers)

GuruKripa participates in selective, ethical affiliate programs (such as Amazon Associates and authorized scriptural publishers). When you purchase recommended sacred texts (e.g., Gita Press Gorakhpur editions) or verified puja essentials through links marked with rel="sponsored", we may earn a small referral commission at absolutely no additional cost to you. This commission directly funds domain registration and zero-cost hosting maintenance.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under CCPA/CPRA, California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected and request its deletion. Because GuruKripa stores all settings strictly inside your device's browser memory (localStorage) and does not maintain a server-side user database, we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

6. Cookieless Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first, cookieless telemetry service that measures aggregate traffic without fingerprinting visitors or tracking IP addresses.

7. Contact & Governance

For inquiries regarding privacy, data portability, or editorial compliance, contact us via our open GitHub repository at github.com/CodingTiger18/GuruKripa.
